PepWorld brings independent online stores and their communities into one simple app, whether it's a local boutique, a home-based food business, a beauty or wellness brand, a hobby shop or any small seller you love.

FOR SHOPPERS
• Open your store's catalog, see photos, options and prices, and order in a few taps
• Pay the way your store accepts and upload your payment receipt right in the app
• Track every order from confirmation to delivery
• Chat directly with the store and get notified when your order is updated
• Join the store's community: announcements, channels and conversations with other customers

FOR STORE OWNERS AND STAFF
• Manage products, photos, variations, prices and stock from your phone
• Get an alert the moment an order comes in, confirm payments and update order status
• Reply to customers in one inbox
• See sales, best sellers and customer insights at a glance
• Give staff their own logins with only the permissions they need

GETTING STARTED
Use the link or code your store shares with you, or sign in with the account you created on the store's website. Stores are run by independent sellers, and each seller is responsible for the products they offer.

Your privacy matters: PepWorld does not show ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.
